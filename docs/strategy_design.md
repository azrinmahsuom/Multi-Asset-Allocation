# Strategy design

Generated by `uv run python -m analysis.pipeline design`; do not edit by hand. Decisions and their reasons are logged in `docs/decisions.md`.

## Investor framing and horizon profiles

Spending money for near-term needs sits outside the strategy; its size is left to the investor. Minimum allocation USD 25,000; daily-tradable ETFs only. Profiles are defined by risk capacity and tested on all 16 JST countries (annual, real, local currency), because the US was the best case in the evidence review (claims 1 and 2). The long profile's drawdown cap is also checked on the monthly SGD backtest; the worse applies. Limits set by the owner on 2026-10-27.

| Profile | Horizon | Purpose | Risk-capacity limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short | about 5 years | a known spend at a set date | Worst real return over any 5-year period, in any country: no worse than -10% cumulative |
| Medium | about 10 years | a flexible goal that could wait a few years | Worst real return over any 10-year period, in any country: no worse than -20% cumulative |
| Long | 20+ years | wealth building with no planned withdrawals | Worst real return over any 20-year period, in any country: at least 0% a year; peak-to-trough fall no worse than -35% |

Selection rule: for each profile, the allocation with the highest expected real return that meets all of its limits.

## Bond sleeve: SGS vs global hedged vs global unhedged

Rules fixed before computing (`analysis.design.bond_options`): three options at matched duration; 60% global equities unhedged in SGD plus 40% bond option, monthly, before costs; ranked on portfolio volatility, maximum drawdown and mean return in the worst 10% of equity months, all in SGD; lowest average rank in the main layer wins.

**Main layer, same fund (2019-07 to 2026-08).** Global government bonds: iShares Global Govt Bond UCITS ETF, unhedged (IGLO.L) and USD-hedged (SGLU.AS) classes. SGS matched to the fund's published effective duration of 6.28 years (SGS maturity 6.4–7.2 years).

![Bond options](../reports/figures/design_bond_options_sgd.png)

**Rule outcome: SGS.** Its portfolio-volatility difference from the runner-up (Global unhedged) has a 95% interval of -0.30% to +0.64% (includes zero). The long layer ranks Global unhedged first, the opposite. Sensitivity of the main-layer winner to the SGS duration: 5.0 years: SGS; 7.5 years: SGS.

**Decision (owner, 2026-10-27): follow the rule; the bond sleeve is SGS, logged as low confidence.** Phase 3 also backtests the global-unhedged sleeve to show how much the choice matters.

Caveats: the main layer is about seven years and includes 2022; the global fund's duration is a current published figure (status to_verify in `reference/bond_durations.csv`); SGS returns are built from benchmark yields, not a traded index; hedges assume covered interest parity; the long layer uses US Treasuries, not global bonds.
